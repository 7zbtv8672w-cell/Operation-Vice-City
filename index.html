About this command centre

PWA · OFFLINE READY

Operation Vice City is a locally stored Progressive Web App. It can be installed on your iPhone Home Screen and opened like an app. The service worker caches the interface for offline use after the first visit. Music links open your selected provider. Xbox, WiZ lighting, temperature and battery status are not directly connected; those checks are manually recorded.
